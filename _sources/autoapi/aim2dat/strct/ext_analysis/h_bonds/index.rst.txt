.. py:function:: calc_hydrogen_bonds(structure: aim2dat.strct.Structure, host_elements='O', bond_threshold=1.25, index_constraint=None, scheme: str = 'baker_hubbard')

   Search for hydrogen bonds.

   :Parameters: * **structure** (*aim2dat.strct.Structure*) -- Structure object.
                * **host_elements** (*list or str*) -- Elements considered as host atoms for hydrogen atoms and hydrogen bonds.
                * **bond_threshold** (*float*) -- Upper threshold to consider the hydrogen atom chemically bonded to the donor.
                * **index_constraint** (*list or None*) -- List of site indices to constrain the search.
                * **scheme** (*str*) -- The applied scheme. Supported options are `'baker_hubbard'`
                  (:doi:`10.1016/0079-6107(84)90007-5`).

   :returns: *tuple* -- Tuple of triples: site of the hydrogen acceptor, hydrogen site, and site of the hydrogen
             donor.
